# Number Allocation Studio
Standalone · works offline

Your numbers, organized

Make groups.
Balance both budgets.

Assign one budget to your chosen numbers and a percentage of it to the remaining numbers. Generate a fresh allocation of every number from 00 to 99.

Configuration

01 / SETUP

Separate with commas, spaces, or new lines. Leave blank to shuffle all numbers equally. Primary numbers are highlighted and spread across groups when possible.

For ₹10,000 and 40%, the targets are ₹10,000 for primary numbers and ₹4,000 for the others. Generated totals may vary by up to 5%.

Group amount preferences may be exceeded when needed to allocate both budgets.

Each number appears exactly once. The two budgets are approximate; actual amounts are shown in the result.
